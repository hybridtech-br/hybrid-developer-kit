# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, act professionally, and focus discussions on the technical work.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, and disclosure of private information are not accepted.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project communication channels.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, or block contributors when necessary to protect the project and its participants.

## Good-faith collaboration

Disagreement is expected. Critique ideas and code, not people. Prefer facts, reproducible evidence, and clear technical reasoning.
